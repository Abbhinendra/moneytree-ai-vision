Proposal for the AI Fullstack Developer round · MoneyTree Realty

A trust layer, not a tech race.

MoneyTree doesn't need to out-build Square Yards or NoBroker's AI infrastructure — it needs an AI layer that makes its 16 years of buyer-first, RERA-verified advisory feel instant, personal, and provably honest. Here's what that looks like, and how I'd build it.

16+ yrs in NCR & pan-India advisory/ 25,000+ deals closed/ 350+ advisors, 10 cities/ 4-state RERA registration

Where MoneyTree stands today

What I looked at before writing a single line of this proposal.

MoneyTree is a channel partner, not a developer — the business is advisory and trust, built across six tracks: residential, commercial, investment strategy, market insights, RERA compliance, and post-purchase support. That last one — RERA compliance — is unusual to see as a named pillar at all; most portals treat it as a filter checkbox, not a service. That's the real asset here, and it's the one an AI layer should protect and amplify, not replace.

What's already live

The site runs a chat widget (Genie) and a WhatsApp click-to-chat line alongside the listings and blog. Genie is a good instinct — a consultative brand should have a first-touch AI concierge. Testing it, the chat does call a model, but the request isn't proxied: the call goes out in a way that would let anyone with browser dev-tools see how it's wired, which is a real risk once it's handling buyer budgets and phone numbers, not just FAQs. That's not a criticism of the product idea — it's the first thing I'd fix, and I've written it up as Pillar 01 below rather than as a bug report, because the fix and the vision are the same piece of work.

The field MoneyTree is actually playing in

Not a feature wishlist — a read on what the well-funded players have already shipped in 2026, so the vision below is additive, not a reinvention of what they've already proven.

Player What they've shipped Where MoneyTree can still win
Square Yards Super Agent Pro — vernacular voice AI handling 200k calls/day; a native ChatGPT storefront; AI valuations & VR tours. Scale, not trust — their AI sells inventory from anyone. MoneyTree can sell AI-assisted, human-verified judgment.
NoBroker ConvoZen — in-house conversational AI analysing ~25M hours of multilingual calls; automated price estimation & listing screening. No brokerage, volume-first — a serious buyer deploying crores still wants an advisor, not just a bot.
Housing.com Native ChatGPT search experience for conversational discovery of listings. Discovery only — nothing on RERA diligence, negotiation, or post-purchase, which is where MoneyTree already competes.
MoneyTree, today Genie widget + static WhatsApp link + a genuinely strong RERA/advisory story that isn't surfaced through any AI touchpoint yet. Everything below.

MoneyTree can't out-spend a platform running 200,000 AI calls a day on Google Cloud infrastructure — and trying to would be the wrong vision to pitch in this round. What it can do is point every one of those AI surfaces at the one thing the giants are structurally weaker at: verified, accountable, RERA-literate advice that a buyer can trust with a multi-crore decision.

So the brief I'd set myself as the AI engineer here isn't "build a chatbot." It's: wherever MoneyTree already does something well by hand — RERA checks, investment reasoning, market notes, advisor follow-up — put an AI layer under it that makes it faster without making it less trustworthy.

Six pieces, mapped to MoneyTree's own six services

Each one extends something that already exists on moneytreerealty.com today, rather than bolting on a separate "AI product."

01
hardens → Genie

Genie v2 — a secured, inventory-grounded advisor

Rebuild the existing widget behind a thin backend proxy so model credentials never touch the browser, with per-session rate limits and logging. Ground its answers with retrieval over MoneyTree's own RERA-verified listings — so it recommends only what the team can actually stand behind, and says "let me connect you to an advisor" the moment a question needs human judgment (pricing negotiation, legal nuance).

Stack: PHP/Laravel · server-side API keys · a small vector index over the listings DB · escalation-to-human as a first-class state, not a fallback.
02
builds on → WhatsApp CTA

WhatsApp AI concierge, in Hindi/Hinglish

Today the WhatsApp button just opens a chat. An AI first-responder — understanding voice notes and Hinglish the way a buyer actually types — can qualify budget, city, and timeline in the first exchange, and hand a warm, pre-briefed lead to a named advisor instead of a cold one. This is the single highest-leverage build: WhatsApp is already where Indian real estate buyers live.

Stack: WhatsApp Business API · speech-to-text for voice notes · a lead-scoring model feeding the advisor's CRM view directly.
03
builds on → RERA Compliance

RERA Diligence Copilot

MoneyTree already manually checks registration, escrow compliance, and litigation history before presenting a project. An OCR + LLM pipeline can pre-read builder documents and state RERA portal data, draft the compliance note an advisor currently writes by hand, and flag anomalies for a human to confirm. This is the pillar competitors aren't building — their AI sells; it doesn't audit.

Stack: document OCR · structured extraction against each state RERA schema · human-in-the-loop sign-off before any note goes to a client.
04
builds on → Investment & Portfolio Strategy

Investment Clarity Engine

A calculator-grade (not black-box) tool that turns a buyer's risk appetite and horizon into a plain-language appreciation and rental-yield read across MoneyTree's shortlisted projects — every number traceable to the comparable sales and supply data the advisory team already pulls together, so it reads as "our analysis," not "an AI estimate."

Stack: a transparent scoring model over listings + locality data · advisor-reviewed before being shown to a client · exportable as the one-pager advisors currently build manually.
05
builds on → Market Insights blog

Content Intelligence for the Market Insights desk

The blog is already strong, original, dated weekly content — rare in this industry. An AI drafting assistant speeds up locality comparisons and auto-produces a Hindi version of every English post (and vice versa), while a named content analyst still edits and ships — keeping the "Team MoneyTree" byline honest.

Stack: drafting assistant wired to internal data/comps · bilingual pass · human edit-and-publish, unchanged from today's workflow.
06
builds on → 350+ advisors

Advisor Copilot

A lightweight internal tool that summarises a client call or WhatsApp thread into next steps, drafts the follow-up message, and scores lead warmth — so a 350-person advisory floor gets some of ConvoZen's leverage without needing NoBroker's infrastructure bill. Internal-first, so it's also the safest place to learn fast and fail cheap.

Stack: call/chat summarisation · CRM-side lead scoring · ships to the sales team before it ever reaches a client.

How it's actually built — the part most vision decks skip

The engineering principle underneath every pillar above: nothing client-facing ever holds a secret.

Browser / WhatsAppno API keys, no model logic
→
Backend proxyauth, rate limits, logging
→
Model APIkey lives server-side only
→
Verified data layerRERA + listings + comps
This is the fix behind Pillar 01, generalised: every pillar above routes through the same backend proxy and the same verified data layer. One secure gateway, six surfaces — not six separate integrations with six separate risk profiles.

A sequence I'd actually commit to

Ordered by what's cheapest to ship and most load-bearing for trust — not by what's flashiest to demo.

Weeks 1–3

Secure the front door

Proxy Genie's model calls server-side, add inventory grounding and human escalation. Stand up the WhatsApp concierge MVP for lead qualification.

Weeks 4–8

Prove the trust story in software

Ship the RERA Diligence Copilot and the Investment Clarity Engine — both advisor-reviewed before anything reaches a client.

Weeks 9–12

Scale what works internally

Roll out the Advisor Copilot to one branch, measure response time and lead-to-visit conversion, then extend bilingual Content Intelligence to the blog desk.

I'm not proposing MoneyTree try to out-spend Square Yards or NoBroker on AI infrastructure — that's not a fight a 2021-founded advisory firm should pick in year one. I'm proposing it make its best differentiator, verified trust, impossible to copy by wiring AI underneath it instead of around it.
